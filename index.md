    <p>Hi, and thank you for visiting my portfolio! I’m an undergraduate mechanical engineering student at Cornell University with solid experience in mechanical design and systems testing and a passion for developing energy efficient systems.</p>

    <p>This past summer I interned in Iceland at the Energy Lab at Reykjavik University, where I conducted performance tests on a model wind turbine using a wind tunnel and designed a CTA probe holder device resistant to vibrations caused by aerodynamic forces.</p>

    <p>At Cornell, I serve as Full Team Lead of AguaClara Cornell, directing R&D initiatives for an engineering team that develops water treatment systems that are entirely powered by gravity. During my time as Ram Pump Subteam Lead, I designed, tested, and led the first-ever successful field deployment of two of our ram pumps in Honduras, a system that uses fluid mechanical principles to pump water vertically without any electricity.</p>

    <p>Outside of engineering, I enjoy playing bass guitar, roller skating, and snowboarding, and one of my main goals in life is to own a pet snake.</p>
