### The AguaClara Vertical Ram Pump (ACVRP)

**Background**  
The purpose of the ACVRP is to automatically deliver clean water treated by the AguaClara plant to the top of the facility without the use of electricity for internal uses, including chemical stock preparation and plumbing support. Currently, this transportation of water is done manually by plant operators, which is both labor- and time-intensive. In 2026, after over 20 years of development, two installations were successfully deployed and operated in Honduras AguaClara facilities by a team of AguaClara students, led by Alma Meili Ren. 

**How it works**  
The final design of the ACVRP is a complex fluid mechanical system that utilizes the conservation of energy, the water hammer effect, and a spring-actuated dual check valve system to generate pressure differentials, driving water up in elevation.
