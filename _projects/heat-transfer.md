Problem set #5, problem 1. 

This assignment evaluates the heat transfer performance of a fin array connected to a heat sink. Modeling a thermal resistance network of the system, I found that the difference in required heat sink temperature with and without the fin array is 88.86 degrees Celsius. This is an important application because it proves that a fin array is a technique that improves system efficiency by reducing energy input. In general, it also broadens the idea that larger surface temperature can improve system energy input, but specific configurations of extended bodies result in higher efficiency.
